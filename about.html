About

Hi, I’m Maria

Baker, business owner, older sister. Building Maria’s Cakerie one cake at a time from a home kitchen in Victoria, BC.

The story so far

I started baking cakes for friends’ birthdays out of my kitchen. One cake turned into two. Two turned into a Tuesday-night side hustle. And a side hustle turned into a real business the day I realized I’d been baking every single weekend for six months straight.

The business used to be called Tina’s Desserts — that was my original brand. Same hands, same recipes, same kitchen. Just a name that finally matched the work I actually wanted to be known for.

Today Maria’s Cakerie & Desserts is a mobile studio serving custom cakes, wedding cakes, dessert tables and treat boxes across the greater Victoria BC area. Every order goes through me directly. Every cake is baked from scratch. Every design is decided between me and the person the cake is for.

Then there’s Carolina

My sister Carolina runs the drinks side of the operation. When we started getting asked "who’s doing the beverages?" at every wedding cake consultation, we decided the answer should be us.

That’s where Two Sisters Social comes in — our mobile drink bar for weddings, showers, corporate events and private parties. Book them alongside the cake or separately; it’s the same phone number either way.

What matters here

Made from scratch. No mixes, no premade shells, no shortcuts. If it’s in a Maria’s Cakerie box, I made it.

Designed with you. No template menus. Every order is a conversation, and the cake is built around your event, your palette, your flavour.

One baker. You’re not talking to a receptionist or a portal. You’re talking to me.
