Cakes & Desserts

Four ways to say celebrate

Every cake is designed with you, baked from scratch by Maria, and delivered anywhere on the Victoria BC area. No factories, no templates.

Custom cakes

Birthdays & celebrations, designed around you

Birthday cakes, anniversary cakes, milestone cakes, "just because" cakes. Send an inspiration image or a colour palette, tell us the flavour, and we’ll build a cake that fits the moment.

Popular flavour combinations: chocolate ganache with fresh raspberries, vanilla bean with lemon curd, red velvet with cream cheese, matcha with white chocolate, tres leches, dulce de leche.

Request a Quote

Wedding cakes

Wedding cakes for Vancouver Island couples

Tiered wedding cakes, sculpted centrepieces, minimalist single-tier designs and everything in between. We take on a limited number of weddings each season so every couple gets a full design consultation, tasting and a cake baked the day of the ceremony.

Book a consultation as early as you can — ideally the same week you lock your venue. Six months out is normal; earlier if your wedding is in peak season.

Plan Your Wedding Cake

Dessert tables

Dessert tables & treat boxes

Styled dessert spreads for weddings, bridal showers, baby showers, corporate events and milestone birthdays. Cupcakes, macarons, mini tarts, cake pops, chocolate-dipped strawberries, custom cookies — combined and styled to match your event colour palette.

Treat boxes are the smaller version: a curated mix of sweets in one beautifully packaged box. Perfect for corporate gifts, party favours or "thank you" moments.

Design a Dessert Table

Chunky cookies

Small-batch, over-the-top cookies

Thick, oversized cookies loaded with the good stuff. Chocolate chunk, brown butter oatmeal, Biscoff, red velvet cheesecake, s’mores, dulce de leche — the menu rotates seasonally.

Sold in mixed boxes of four, six or twelve, and single-flavour orders for events. Great as party favours, corporate gifts, or a Sunday-night indulgence.
