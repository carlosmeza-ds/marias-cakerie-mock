FAQ

The honest questions

How far in advance should I book?

Custom cakes and dessert tables are best booked 3-4 weeks out. Wedding cakes: six months minimum, ideally at the same time you book your venue. Two Sisters Social is booking events season-by-season.

Confirm lead-time policy with Maria.
Is there a physical shop I can visit?

Maria’s Cakerie is mobile-only. Every order is baked to order, and we coordinate pickup or delivery in the greater Victoria BC area directly with you.

Can you handle allergies and dietary preferences?

Yes — gluten-free, dairy-free and vegan options are available on request. Please flag every allergy in your inquiry so we can plan the recipe and the kitchen accordingly.

Confirm dietary options actually offered.
How do I get a quote?

Send an inquiry through the contact form or WhatsApp with the date, guest count and any inspiration images you have. Maria will follow up within 24 hours with a quote and next steps.

What about drinks for my event?

That’s where Two Sisters Social comes in — our mobile drink bar for weddings, showers, corporate events and private parties. Book them alongside your cake for a full sweet-and-drinks experience.

Ready to talk cake?

Tell us about your date, your occasion and the vibe you’re going for. Maria takes it from there.
