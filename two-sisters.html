Introducing Two Sisters Social

Every unforgettable celebration deserves an unforgettable experience

Two Sisters Social is a mobile drink bar created to bring elegance, fun, and interactive moments to your special events. From handcrafted mocktails to refreshing signature beverages, every drink is made to impress your guests and elevate your celebration.

Built for

Every kind of celebration

Weddings

Cocktail-hour mocktails, signature drinks named for the couple, styled bar setup.

Corporate

Branded beverage stations for company events, product launches and holiday parties.

Showers

Bridal showers, baby showers, engagement parties. Interactive, photo-ready, non-alcoholic-first.

Private events

Birthdays, anniversaries, milestone celebrations, intimate gatherings.

What to expect

More than a drink station — a moment

Our beautifully styled bar is designed to be more than just drinks. It’s the corner of your event guests gather around, the backdrop of half your photos, the thing they mention on the drive home.

Every setup is styled to complement your event colour palette. Every drink is made in front of your guests. Every menu is co-designed with you.

Here’s to creating beautiful moments, one drink at a time. 🥂🤍

Meet the sisters

Maria & Carolina

Two Sisters Social is exactly what it sounds like: two biological sisters running a drink bar together. Maria bakes the cakes at Maria’s Cakerie. Carolina brings the drinks.

The idea started at a wedding where a client asked "who’s doing the drinks?" and neither of us wanted the answer to be "a rented setup with a stranger behind it." So we built our own.
