FAQ

Wedding & event questions

How far in advance should I book my wedding cake?

Six months minimum, ideally at the same time you book your venue. Peak wedding season (May-September in Victoria) fills up fastest.

Do you do consultations and tastings?

Yes. Every wedding client gets a full consultation and a tasting box with four flavour options. We schedule these once you’ve confirmed your date.

How many guests can you serve?

Wedding cakes are sized to your guest count, from intimate 20-guest ceremonies to 200-plus receptions. Dessert tables scale similarly.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes — delivery, on-site cake assembly and dessert-table styling are all included in the wedding package. Delivery fees depend on the venue location on Vancouver Island.

Can you handle a full corporate event?

Yes. Corporate branding on cookies, branded dessert tables, and coordination with the Two Sisters Social drink bar for full sweet-and-beverage service.
